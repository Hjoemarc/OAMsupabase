# 07 Domain: Criteria & Standards

## 1. Domain Entities
* **Area / Standard**: Major dimension (Curriculum & Instruction, Faculty, Facilities, Laboratories, Library, Administration).
* **Benchmark / Parameter**: Discrete requirement under an Area.
* **Scoring Rubric**: Scale (1 to 5 or percentage) defining compliance threshold.

## 2. Invariants
* Areas belong to an Accrediting Body template or specific Cycle.
* Benchmarks cannot be deleted once evidence has been attached to them.
